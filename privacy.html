Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Your privacy is simple: Mendvolt does not collect, use, share, or sell personal data.

Overview

Mendvolt is developed and published by yunpeng. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Mendvolt mobile game.

Data Collection

Mendvolt does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or any other data that can identify you. The game does not require an account.

Data Sharing

Because Mendvolt does not collect personal data, it does not share personal data with third parties. Mendvolt does not use third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics services.

Local Game Data

Your game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device so that the game can remember your preferences and progress. This local data is not transmitted to yunpeng or to any third party by the game.

Children's Privacy

Mendvolt does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. No personal information is collected through the game.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and the “Last updated” date will be revised.

Contact

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy, please contact yunpeng through the support channel listed on the App Store product page.
